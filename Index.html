How Typing Speed & Mistakes are Calculated in India

Official guidelines followed by SSC CGL/CHSL, Railway RRB NTPC, High Courts, and CPCT exams.

Gross Words Per Minute (GWPM)

In standard typing conventions, 5 key depressions (keystrokes) are counted as one single word. Gross speed measures raw speed without deducting mistakes.

GWPM = (Total Character Keystrokes ÷ 5) ÷ Total Time in Minutes

Net Words Per Minute (NWPM)

Net speed reflects accurate typing and penalizes wrong words typed within the given duration.

NWPM = GWPM - (Total Error Words ÷ Total Time in Minutes)

Full Mistakes vs Half Mistakes

  • Full Mistakes (100% deduction): Omission of a word, addition of an unprovided word, or complete substitution.
  • Half Mistakes (50% deduction): Spelling errors, improper uppercase/lowercase capitalization, or punctuation spacing flaws.

5% Ignorable Mistakes Rule

Government recruitment commissions usually tolerate up to 5% errors of total words typed. Deductions are only applied to mistakes exceeding this 5% quota.
